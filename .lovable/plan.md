# Make EZRP’s purpose and map clearer

## Goal
Make the home page explain, at a glance, that EZRP helps people navigate ERP and digital transformation safely and practically.

## Changes
- Replace the current opening copy with a plain-English EZRP purpose statement focused on reducing complexity, avoidable pitfalls, and expensive mistakes.
- Add a concise ERP primer: what ERP is, what digital transformation adds, and the main areas involved across people, process, data, technology, governance, and change.
- Add a short “how to use this library” section with clear entry points for browsing the field guide, following a value stream, or opening a specific topic.
- Link the field guide call-to-action to the existing guide library.
- Rework the map into a more organic Clariti-style brain network while preserving the hierarchy: EZRP at the centre, business domains around it, value streams branching outward, and guide topics beyond them.
- Show cross-cutting capabilities as a separate connected layer, with visible links into the domains and streams they support.
- Keep the existing EZRP colours, typography, navigation, guide content, and Alpha label.

## Technical details
- Limit the work to the home page, map component, and existing map styles.
- Reuse existing typed domain, stream, guide, and cross-cutting relationship data.
- Keep pan, zoom, selection, branch highlighting, keyboard access, and reduced-motion support.
- Verify the home page and map at desktop and mobile sizes, plus navigation and build health.
