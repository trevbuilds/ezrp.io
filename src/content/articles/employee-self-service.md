## Why it matters

Self-service is usually justified on cost and delivers on accuracy: the employee knows their own address and bank account, and the HR administrator does not.

- **It shortens the path**: Fewer hand-offs between the person who knows and the record that holds it.
- **It moves risk**: Bank detail changes are the obvious one, and need verification regardless of who enters them.
- **It exposes the process**: Anything that cannot be self-served is usually a process that was never properly defined.

## The workflow

```steps
Authentication :: The employee is identified, with the assurance the transaction warrants.
Transaction :: A change is submitted — details, leave, timesheet, expense.
Validation :: The system checks entitlement, format and policy before accepting.
Approval :: Where needed, the manager approves against a clear view of the impact.
Update :: The record changes, effective dated, with an audit trail.
Confirmation :: The employee sees the outcome, and is notified if something is rejected.
```

> Bank detail changes should trigger verification even when self-served. Self-service moves the keystroke, not the fraud risk — and an account change made from a compromised account is the payroll equivalent of invoice redirection.

## What you need in place

- **Mobile access**: A shift workforce is not sitting at a desk.
- **Clear entitlement display**: Leave balances visible at the point of request.
- **Manager visibility**: Approvers see coverage and cost impact, not just a request.
- **Verification on sensitive changes**: Bank details and identity documents.
- **A fallback path**: Not everyone can self-serve, and the exception route has to exist by design.

## Questions to ask

```qa
Adoption :: What share of eligible transactions are actually self-served? :: Low adoption means the parallel manual path is still open.
Sensitive changes :: What extra verification applies to bank details? :: Notification to the previous contact details at minimum.
Mobile :: Can a shift worker do this from a phone, offline? :: If not, adoption in operational areas will stay low.
Rejection :: Does an employee find out when something is rejected, and why? :: Silent rejection is how trust in the system is lost.
Accessibility :: Does it work for the whole workforce? :: Including those with limited digital access or literacy.
```

## Metrics and KPIs to track

```metrics
Self-service adoption :: Share of eligible transactions completed by employees.
Transaction cycle time :: Submission to effective change.
Rejection rate :: Submissions rejected, by reason.
Support contacts :: Queries raised per transaction type, a measure of clarity.
```
