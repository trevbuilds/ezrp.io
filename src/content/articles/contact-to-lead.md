## Why it matters

Everything downstream inherits the quality of this stage. A pipeline full of unqualified leads does not just waste sales time, it corrupts the forecast that operations and finance are planning against.

- **Channel coverage**: Interest arrives by web, event, referral, campaign and inbound call. If one channel bypasses the system, its leads are invisible.
- **Consent**: Capturing consent at the point of contact is what makes later marketing lawful, and in Australia that obligation sits with the sender.
- **Scoring discipline**: A scoring model agreed with sales is the difference between qualification and guesswork.

## The workflow

```steps
Channel capture :: Interest is collected from every channel into one place, with its source recorded.
Consent and contact creation :: A contact record is created, carrying what the person agreed to be sent.
Interaction tracking :: Subsequent behaviour is logged against that contact rather than against a campaign in isolation.
Lead scoring :: The contact is scored on engagement and fit using criteria sales has signed off.
Lead qualification :: Above the agreed threshold, the contact becomes a lead and moves on.
```

> Decide what a qualified lead means before you configure scoring. Teams that configure first end up tuning a number nobody agrees on, and sales quietly reverts to its own list.

## What you need in place

- **One contact record**: Deduplicated, so the same person arriving twice does not become two leads.
- **Source attribution**: Every lead carries where it came from, or you cannot judge a channel later.
- **Consent management**: Recorded, timestamped, and honoured on unsubscribe.
- **An agreed scoring model**: Owned jointly by marketing and sales, and revisited against real conversion data.

## Metrics and KPIs to track

```metrics
Lead volume by channel :: New leads captured, split by source.
Qualification rate :: Share of captured contacts that reach qualified status.
Cost per qualified lead :: Channel spend divided by qualified leads it produced.
Duplicate rate :: Share of new contacts that were already known.
```
