## Why it matters

Onboarding crosses more systems than almost any other HR process — HR, payroll, identity, facilities, learning — and each hand-off is a place it can stall.

- **It is visible**: Every failure is experienced personally by someone forming their first impression.
- **It is time-bound**: Day one arrives whether the provisioning finished or not.
- **It sets up compliance**: Right to work, qualifications and mandatory training are captured here or not at all.

## The workflow

```steps
Offer acceptance :: The candidate accepts and becomes a pending employee.
Record creation :: The employee record is created against an approved position.
Pre-boarding :: Documents, right to work, bank and super details, and mandatory forms collected before day one.
Provisioning :: Identity, access, equipment and workspace prepared, triggered by the HR record.
Day one :: Induction, safety, systems access, and a manager who knows they are coming.
Probation and follow-up :: Check-ins scheduled, mandatory training tracked to completion.
```

> Provisioning should be triggered by the HR record, not by an email to IT. Where it is an email, some proportion never gets sent, and nobody finds out until the person arrives.

## What you need in place

- **Right to work verification**: Captured and re-checked where visas expire.
- **Automated provisioning**: Access derived from the position, not requested ad hoc.
- **Pre-boarding collection**: Bank, super choice and tax declaration before the first pay run.
- **Mandatory training tracking**: With a consequence for non-completion.
- **Manager preparation**: The receiving manager prompted before, not on, day one.

## Questions to ask

```qa
Trigger :: What starts provisioning — a record or a request? :: A request is a step that can be skipped.
Day-one readiness :: What share of starters have pay, access and equipment on day one? :: Measure it; assumptions here are usually generous.
Right to work :: How are expiring work rights monitored? :: An expired visa is an employment law problem, not an HR admin one.
First pay :: Does the starter make the first pay run after their start date? :: Missing it is common and badly received.
Training :: Is mandatory training tracked to completion or just assigned? :: Assignment is not evidence.
```

## Metrics and KPIs to track

```metrics
Day-one readiness :: Starters with pay, access and equipment ready.
Time to productivity :: Start to independent performance, by role family.
Mandatory training completion :: Within the required window.
Early attrition :: Leavers within the first six months.
```
