## Why it matters

The cost of an unrecorded decision is not the meeting — it is the rework when a design is built on an assumption someone else already overturned.

- **It prevents re-litigation**: A recorded decision with a rationale ends the argument.
- **It makes accountability real**: Actions with an owner and a date either complete or visibly do not.
- **It is the audit trail**: Especially for anything with a financial or probity dimension.

## The workflow

```steps
Capture :: Recorded in the meeting, with owner and date, not reconstructed afterwards.
Classify :: Decision or action, and which forum owns it.
Record rationale :: Why, what was considered, and what it depends on.
Assign :: One named owner. Shared ownership is no ownership.
Track :: Reviewed at each forum, with ageing visible.
Close :: Evidence of completion, not an assertion.
```

> Record what the decision assumed. When an assumption later proves wrong, the decisions that rest on it can be found and revisited — without the assumption recorded, they cannot.

## What you need in place

- **One register**: Across the programme, not one per workstream.
- **Capture in the room**: Minutes written later lose the rationale.
- **Single named owners**: With dates that mean something.
- **Ageing visibility**: Overdue items surfaced automatically rather than at review.
- **A link to design artefacts**: So a decision can be traced to what it changed.

## Questions to ask

```qa
Single register :: Is there one register, or several? :: Several means conflicting answers to the same question.
Rationale :: Do we record why, or only what? :: Only what is a list; why is a record.
Ownership :: Does every action have one named owner? :: Team ownership means nobody does it.
Ageing :: How old is the oldest open action? :: The answer is usually uncomfortable and always informative.
Closure evidence :: What proves an action is complete? :: Self-declared closure hides incomplete work.
```

## Metrics and KPIs to track

```metrics
Open actions by age :: Particularly those past their due date.
Overdue rate :: Share of actions closed after their date.
Decisions reopened :: Indicates the wrong forum or missing rationale.
Capture rate :: Decisions recorded against decisions taken, sampled.
```
