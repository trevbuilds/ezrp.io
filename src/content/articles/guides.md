## Why it matters

Most organisations start an ERP programme on a business case built from a vendor conversation. These three assessments exist to produce an independent starting position before that happens.

- **A health check tells you whether replacement is the answer**: Often the problem is process, data or governance, and a new system inherits all three.
- **A readiness checklist tells you whether now is the time**: Capacity, sponsorship and data condition decide this more than strategy does.
- **A selection method tells you what to buy**: Against your own requirements, weighted before demonstrations begin.

## The three assessments

```steps
ERP health check :: Where the current estate stands — function, data, integration, support, cost and risk.
Digital transformation readiness checklist :: Whether the organisation can absorb the change it is proposing.
Finding the one :: Selecting a product and an implementation partner against weighted, evidenced criteria.
```

> Do them in that order. Selecting a product before assessing readiness produces a good product and a programme the organisation cannot resource.

## What you need in place

- **Honest input**: The assessments are only as good as the willingness to record what is actually true.
- **A named owner**: Someone who will act on the output.
- **Evidence over opinion**: Counts, ages, costs and volumes, not impressions.
- **A decision that follows**: An assessment with no decision attached becomes a document.

## Questions to ask

```qa
Problem :: What problem are we actually solving? :: If it can be stated in one sentence, the scope will follow; if not, it will sprawl.
Alternative :: What happens if we do nothing for two years? :: The honest answer shapes urgency and scope.
Capacity :: Who would do this work, and what would they stop doing? :: The most common reason good programmes fail.
Evidence :: Are our answers based on data or on impression? :: Impressions are consistently more optimistic.
```

## Metrics and KPIs to track

```metrics
Assessment currency :: Time since the last health check.
Action closure :: Findings with an owner and a completion date.
Readiness gaps closed :: Before the programme starts rather than during.
```
