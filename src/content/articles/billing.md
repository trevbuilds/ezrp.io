## Why it matters

Work that is delivered but not billed is revenue lost, not deferred. Work billed incorrectly is a dispute that delays the cash and damages the relationship.

- **It depends on capture**: Unrecorded time cannot be billed.
- **It depends on terms**: Fixed price, time and materials, milestone and capped arrangements bill differently.
- **It drives recognition**: When revenue is recognised is an accounting policy question, not a billing convenience.

## The workflow

```steps
Billable identification :: Recorded time and expense assessed against the contract terms.
Review :: A person reviews what will be billed before the client sees it.
Invoice generation :: The invoice is produced with the detail the client's process requires.
Dispute handling :: Queries worked without stalling the rest of the receivable.
Revenue recognition :: Revenue recognised per policy — on delivery, on milestone, or over time.
Work in progress management :: Unbilled work aged and actioned rather than accumulating.
```

> Ageing work in progress is where professional services firms lose money without noticing. Unbilled time past ninety days is rarely recovered in full.

## What you need in place

- **Contract terms as data**: Rates, caps, milestones and what is billable held in the system, not in the engagement letter alone.
- **Timely time capture**: Weekly at worst; monthly makes billing archaeological.
- **Pre-bill review**: By someone who knows the client relationship.
- **Client-ready invoice format**: Purchase order references and the detail their payables team requires.
- **Recognition rules**: Configured to the policy, including for fixed-price arrangements.

## Questions to ask

```qa
WIP ageing :: How much unbilled work is over sixty days? :: Past that, recovery drops sharply.
Terms :: Are contract rates and caps in the system? :: If they live in a document, billing will diverge from the agreement.
Review :: Who checks a bill before it goes? :: Unreviewed invoices generate disputes that cost more than the review.
Recognition :: Does recognition follow billing, or policy? :: They are not the same, and conflating them misstates revenue.
Write-offs :: Who approves them, and is the reason recorded? :: The reasons are the improvement backlog.
```

## Metrics and KPIs to track

```metrics
Work in progress :: Value unbilled, by age.
Realisation rate :: Billed value against recorded value.
Days to invoice :: Work completed to invoice issued.
Write-off value :: With reasons.
Dispute rate :: Invoices queried, by cause.
```
