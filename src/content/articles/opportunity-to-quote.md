## Why it matters

Quoting is the stage most ERP sales configuration actually turns on. Pricing rules, discount authority and approval thresholds all land here, and a quote that the business cannot honour is worse than a slow one.

## The workflow

```steps
Opportunity assessment :: Sales judge whether the opportunity is ready to be quoted, or needs more work first.
Needs confirmation :: What the customer actually requires is confirmed, not assumed from the original enquiry.
Solution configuration :: Products, services and options are configured into a valid combination.
Pricing :: Price is derived from the configuration, with discount applied inside approval limits.
Quote issue :: The quote is issued — raised by the rep, or self-served by the customer.
```

> Configure, price and quote capability earns its cost when the product set is genuinely configurable. For a simple catalogue it is overhead. Judge it on how many invalid combinations a rep could otherwise sell.

## What you need in place

- **A validated product model**: Rules about what can be sold with what, so invalid configurations cannot be quoted.
- **Pricing rules**: Price lists, customer-specific pricing and volume breaks held as data, not in a spreadsheet.
- **Delegation of authority**: Discount limits configured to mirror the signed authority, with escalation above them.
- **Quote versioning**: Revisions tracked, so what the customer agreed to is unambiguous later.
- **Margin visibility**: The rep can see the margin effect of a discount at the point of offering it.

## Questions to ask

```qa
Configuration :: Can the system stop a rep quoting a combination we cannot deliver? :: Test with a deliberately invalid configuration rather than a clean one.
Approvals :: How are discount approvals enforced and escalated? :: Confirm the limits are configuration, not training, and that they match the signed authority.
Quote to order fidelity :: Does the order carry exactly what the quote agreed? :: Follow one quote through to a booked order and compare line by line.
```

## Metrics and KPIs to track

```metrics
Quote turnaround time :: Elapsed time from opportunity to issued quote.
Quote-to-order conversion :: Share of quotes that become orders.
Average discount :: Discount given against list, by segment and by rep.
Approval cycle time :: Time quotes spend waiting on an approver.
Quote revision count :: Revisions per won deal — a proxy for how well needs were confirmed.
```
