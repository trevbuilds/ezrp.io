## The receivables cycle

```steps
Customer setup and credit assessment :: Credit limit and payment terms established before trading, not after the first default.
Billing :: Invoice raised from the order, contract, meter reading or milestone.
Delivery of the invoice :: Email, portal, e-invoicing network or post — whichever the customer will actually action.
Payment receipt :: Funds arrive by direct debit, card, EFT or BPAY.
Cash application :: Receipts matched to open invoices, ideally automatically by remittance data.
Collections :: Overdue accounts worked through a defined escalation path.
Dispute resolution :: Queried invoices investigated, then credited or upheld with a reason recorded.
Revenue recognition and reporting :: Revenue recognised on the correct basis, and the aged debtors report reconciled to the ledger.
```

## Invoice accuracy is a collections strategy

Most late payment is not refusal to pay. It is a customer waiting on a corrected invoice, a missing purchase order reference, or an invoice sent to the wrong person. These are billing defects wearing a collections costume.

- **Capture their PO reference**: Many corporate customers cannot pay an invoice that lacks one. Make it a mandatory field at order entry.
- **Bill to the right contact**: The person who signed the contract is rarely the person who processes invoices.
- **Match their format**: Large customers often mandate a portal or e-invoicing network. Fighting it delays payment.
- **Invoice promptly**: The clock starts at issue, not at delivery. Every day of billing lag is a day of DSO.

## Credit and collections

- **Risk-based credit limits**: Limits set from credit data and trading history, reviewed rather than set once.
- **Automated dunning**: Escalating reminders triggered by age, with tone and channel varying by segment.
- **Credit holds**: System-enforced stops on new orders past a threshold, with a defined release authority.
- **Dispute codes**: Every held invoice carries a reason code, so the root causes can be reported and fixed.
- **Provisioning and write-off**: Doubtful debt provisioned on a consistent policy, with write-off requiring approval.

> Segment before you automate. Chasing a strategic account with the same automated sequence used for small accounts is how finance systems damage commercial relationships.

## Metrics and KPIs to track

```metrics
Days sales outstanding :: Average days from invoice to cash received.
Billing lag :: Days from delivery or milestone to invoice issued.
Aged debtors profile :: Balance by ageing bucket, and the trend in the oldest buckets.
Cash application auto-match rate :: Receipts allocated without manual intervention.
Dispute rate and value :: Invoices queried as a share of invoices issued.
Bad debt write-off :: Written off as a percentage of revenue.
Collection effectiveness index :: Cash collected against what was available to collect.
```
