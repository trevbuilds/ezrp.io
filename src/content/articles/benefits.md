## Why it matters

Benefits carry tax consequences that fall on the employer, and eligibility rules that vary by employment type. Getting them wrong produces an FBT liability nobody budgeted for.

- **FBT is the employer's**: Not the employee's, and it is assessed on a different year to income tax.
- **Eligibility varies**: Full-time, part-time, casual and fixed-term employees are frequently treated differently.
- **Packaging affects pay**: Salary sacrifice changes gross, which changes tax, super treatment and leave calculations.

## The workflow

```steps
Plan design :: What is offered, to whom, and on what terms.
Eligibility :: Employees matched to the benefits their employment type and tenure allow.
Enrolment :: Elections captured, with effective dates and any waiting periods.
Payroll integration :: Deductions and packaging applied to the pay run correctly.
FBT tracking :: Reportable benefits accumulated through the FBT year.
Reporting :: FBT return prepared, and reportable fringe benefits amounts reported on income statements.
```

> Salary sacrifice into superannuation is treated differently from packaging a car. Both reduce take-home pay; only one creates an FBT exposure. Configure them separately and label them clearly.

## What you need in place

- **Eligibility rules as data**: Not as a policy document someone interprets.
- **Effective-dated elections**: So mid-year changes calculate correctly.
- **FBT categorisation**: Each benefit type mapped to its treatment.
- **Reportable benefits tracking**: Feeding income statements at year end.
- **A provider integration**: Where packaging is outsourced, a reconciliation between their records and payroll.

## Questions to ask

```qa
FBT exposure :: Which benefits create FBT, and is anyone tracking the running total? :: Discovering it at year end is the expensive way.
Eligibility :: Are rules enforced by the system or by whoever processes the form? :: Inconsistency here becomes a discrimination question.
Packaging :: Does salary sacrifice calculate correctly against super and leave? :: Sacrifice interacts with OTE, and errors compound.
Provider :: If packaging is outsourced, how do their records reconcile to ours? :: Two sets of numbers, one payroll.
Termination :: What happens to a novated lease or packaged benefit when someone leaves? :: Decide before, not during.
```

## Metrics and KPIs to track

```metrics
Benefit uptake :: Enrolment by benefit and employment type.
FBT liability :: Accumulated through the year, against budget.
Payroll deduction errors :: Corrections to benefit deductions per period.
Provider reconciliation variance :: Differences between provider and payroll records.
```
