## Why it matters

- **Lead nurturing**: Moves prospects toward a buying decision without a person chasing each one.
- **Productivity**: Automates the repetitive send-and-follow-up work that otherwise consumes a marketing team.
- **Measurable campaigns**: Produces analytics on campaigns and engagement, so spend can be argued for with evidence.
- **One view of the customer**: Sales, marketing and service see the same interaction history.

## The workflow

This sequence sits entirely inside the Contact to Lead stage, and ends the moment a qualified lead is handed to sales.

```steps
Lead capture :: Collect detail from landing pages, social channels and events, and store it against the CRM record.
Segmentation :: Classify leads by behaviour, demographics and engagement.
Campaign execution :: Run targeted campaigns against those segments through automated workflows.
Lead nurturing :: Deliver relevant content at the right point to move the lead along.
Evaluation and scoring :: Score leads on engagement and likelihood to convert, so follow-up is prioritised.
Handover to sales :: Pass qualified leads to the sales team inside the CRM for personal follow-up.
Analysis and reporting :: Measure campaign effectiveness and adjust.
```

> Lead scoring is where marketing and sales either agree or quietly stop trusting each other. Define the scoring criteria jointly, and revisit them once real conversion data exists.

## What you need in place

- **CRM integration**: The automation tool has to read and write the same customer data the CRM holds.
- **Email and channel tools**: Automated communication triggered by customer action rather than by calendar.
- **Lead management**: Capture, track and manage leads across their whole lifecycle.
- **Segmentation**: Build detailed segments from CRM data rather than from exported spreadsheets.
- **Lead scoring**: An agreed model for prioritising leads by engagement and fit.

## Questions to ask vendors

```qa
Integration :: How does the tool integrate with our existing CRM? :: Confirm whether the integration is native, and what it costs to keep working.
Scoring model :: Can we customise lead scoring to fit our business? :: Check whether scoring rules can be changed by marketing or need a developer.
Journey handling :: How does the tool handle nurturing across stages of the customer journey? :: Walk a real journey through the product rather than accepting a diagram.
Attribution :: How do we measure ROI of automation efforts? :: Establish what attribution model is available and whether finance will accept it.
```

## Metrics and KPIs to track

```metrics
Conversion rate :: Share of leads that become customers.
Lead generation volume :: Number of new leads captured.
Engagement rate :: How actively leads interact with campaigns.
Campaign ROI :: Return on investment for a given marketing effort.
Customer lifetime value :: Total value a customer is expected to bring over the relationship.
```
