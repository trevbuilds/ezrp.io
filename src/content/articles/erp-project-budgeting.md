## Why it matters

Software and implementation partner fees are the visible costs and usually the minority. The costs that overrun are the internal ones, because they are not invoiced and so were never estimated.

- **Internal effort is the largest omission**: Backfill for the subject matter experts a programme takes out of the business is a real cost that rarely appears.
- **Data work is consistently underestimated**: Cleansing is a business activity, not a technical one, and it is discovered rather than planned.
- **The run cost changes permanently**: Subscription, support, and the capability to operate it.

## The workflow

```steps
Scope definition :: What is in, and explicitly what is not, before any number is produced.
Cost modelling :: Software, implementation, internal effort, backfill, data, integration, change, training and infrastructure.
Contingency :: Sized against assessed risk, held centrally, released against evidence.
Business case :: Costs against benefits, with named benefit owners and a baseline.
Approval :: Through the organisation's own capital or investment process.
Tracking :: Actuals and commitments against baseline, with forecast to complete.
Benefit realisation :: Tracked after go-live by the owners, not by the programme.
```

> Budget the year after go-live. Hypercare, defect remediation, the deferred scope and the first period-end problems all land after the implementation budget was assumed to have closed.

## What you need in place

- **A cost model including internal effort**: Priced at a real rate, with backfill.
- **Contingency held centrally**: Contingency distributed into workstreams is spent.
- **Capital and operating split**: Determined early, with finance, because it changes the approval path and the accounting.
- **A benefits baseline**: Measured before, or the benefit cannot be evidenced after.
- **Forecast to complete, refreshed**: Spend to date tells you nothing on its own.
- **Post-go-live funding**: Explicitly in the envelope.

## Questions to ask

```qa
Internal cost :: Is internal effort in the budget, at a real rate? :: Usually not, and it is usually the largest line.
Backfill :: Who does the day job while the expert is on the programme? :: If the answer is nobody, expect both to suffer.
Data :: What is budgeted for cleansing? :: It is business effort, it is large, and it is discovered late.
Capitalisation :: What can be capitalised, and who decided? :: It changes the approval route and the reported result.
Contingency :: How much, held by whom, released on what? :: Contingency inside workstream budgets is not contingency.
After go-live :: What is funded for the twelve months after? :: If nothing, the programme ends when the problems start.
Benefits :: Who owns each benefit, and what is the baseline? :: Unowned benefits are not realised, and unbaselined ones cannot be proven.
```

## Metrics and KPIs to track

```metrics
Spend against baseline :: Actual and committed, by category.
Forecast to complete :: Refreshed on a cadence, with variance explained.
Contingency drawdown :: Consumed against elapsed schedule.
Internal effort :: Days consumed against planned, by business area.
Benefit realisation :: Measured against baseline, by owner, after go-live.
Run cost :: Ongoing cost against the business case assumption.
```
