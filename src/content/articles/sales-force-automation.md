## Why it matters in an ERP context

Integrating sales automation into the ERP makes sales activity visible across the whole organisation, and keeps information flowing between sales, finance, inventory and service. Without it, the sales pipeline is a forecast nobody else can verify.

## The workflow

```steps
Lead management :: Capture leads from their various sources, track activity, and manage qualification.
Contact and account management :: Hold detailed contact and account records, including communication history and related opportunities.
Opportunity management :: Track opportunities from creation to closure, with stages, activities and probabilities.
Quote and order management :: Generate quotes against customer requirements and convert them to orders once approved.
Sales forecasting :: Use historical data and predictive analysis to forecast, which drives planning and resourcing.
Task and activity management :: Organise sales activity, schedule tasks and set follow-up reminders.
Performance management :: Monitor performance against target and report on it.
```

> Quoting is the stage most ERP sales configuration actually turns on — pricing rules, discount authority and approval limits all land here. It is worth more design time than lead capture.

## What you need in place

- **Marketing automation integration**: So leads transition from marketing to sales without a manual rekey.
- **Customisation and configuration**: Match the system to the sales process, workflows and reporting the business runs.
- **Data management and migration**: Clean, accurate and complete data moved from the existing system.
- **User training and adoption**: Sales teams need to see the system pay them back, or they will keep their own spreadsheet.
- **Security and access controls**: Access rights that protect commercially sensitive pipeline information.

## Implementing it

```steps
Requirement analysis :: Define the sales process requirements, reporting needs and integration points.
Solution selection :: Choose against those requirements, weighing scale and customisation.
System design and customisation :: Design layout, customise fields and configure workflows.
Data migration :: Move existing sales data with integrity checks.
Integration :: Connect to the other ERP modules and any external systems.
Training and go-live :: Train users and go live with support in place for the first weeks.
```

## Metrics and KPIs to track

```metrics
Sales cycle length :: Average time from lead capture to closed deal.
Conversion rate :: Share of leads that convert to sales.
Average deal size :: Average revenue per closed deal.
Pipeline health :: Total value of opportunities by stage.
Sales rep performance :: Individual performance against target.
```
